# Invoicing (plan step 0.7b)

The invoicing entity is an open owner decision, due by day 14. There are two options: invoice as the existing business behind zovo.one, or register a JDG (jednoosobowa dzialalnosc gospodarcza). Until the owner decides, the Stripe payment links collect prepayment, and each invoice goes out within 7 days of the decision. The links live in Stripe account acct_1MmUPwJKCamubEm1, a Polish individual account whose display name is BeLikeNative. That account's card statement descriptor currently reads "AUTOM8 LLC", so the name on the invoices should match whatever the owner picks. When the decision is made, STATUS.md records the entity name and VAT ID, and the first invoice PDF is stored in state/invoices/.

VAT treatment is set by where the client is. For an EU business client with a valid VAT ID (check it in VIES before invoicing), the invoice shows no Polish VAT and carries the line "Reverse charge: VAT to be accounted for by the recipient under Article 196 of Council Directive 2006/112/EC (odwrotne obciazenie)", plus the client's VAT ID. Supplying these services to EU businesses usually requires a VAT-UE (NIP-UE) registration, even for a business that is otherwise VAT-exempt. The accountant should confirm this before the first EU invoice. For a client outside the EU, the service is supplied outside Poland, so no Polish VAT is charged, and the invoice says "Not subject to Polish VAT, place of supply outside Poland (art. 28b ustawy o VAT)".
