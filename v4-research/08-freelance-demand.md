## 6. Synthesis for the operator (cold read)

1. There is no open, countable freelance demand stream naming Medusa, Strapi, Directus, Payload, Supabase, or the other 15 products on any board reachable without login. The single measurable board (n8n community Jobs) shows roughly one demand post per two days and 80+ applicants per post. Product expertise on a public board is not a differentiator at that ratio.
2. The vendor channels that do route leads are agency-gated: Medusa Experts requires an agency name, a live Medusa Cloud project, and a declared project budget (options start at $1k-$5k, listed experts declare $5k-$250k+ floors); n8n Experts is a closed pilot for agencies with 3+ paying n8n customers in UK/IE, Northern Europe or North America; Strapi routes leads to "Community, Business, and Enterprise partners" with no public request form. A solo Polish developer with merged i18n PRs matches none of the published entry criteria today.
3. Medusa specifically: 11-50 staff, ~$9M raised as of 2022, 30 agency Experts, intros brokered by Medusa staff through a form that asks for order volume. Five merged translation PRs are not a listed criterion; "launched one or more projects on Medusa Cloud" is.
4. Published rate data for these products is either blocked (Upwork, Toptal, Clutch, Malt) or templated marketing (Arc's "$60-100+/hour" appears verbatim for Strapi, Supabase, Payload and even an "NFT developer" page). The only hard numbers are forum asks of USD 250-500 per n8n workflow and one $6,000/mo full-time n8n consultant listing.
5. Implication: the OSS-contribution record would need to be converted into either (a) a live Medusa Cloud project plus an agency identity to qualify for the Experts directory, or (b) a subcontracting relationship with one of the 30 listed agencies (Rigby is in Poland; see rigbyjs.com link on its expert card), rather than into direct marketplace demand.
