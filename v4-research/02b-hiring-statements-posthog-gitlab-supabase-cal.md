# Hiring-from-community statements: PostHog, GitLab, Supabase, Cal.com (fetched 2026-09-24)

## PostHog
- No hire-from-contributors statement. https://posthog.com/handbook/people/talent: "for engineers, consider reaching out via GitHub (if they're active contributors)". CONTRIBUTING.md: "We sometimes reward helpful contributions with merch even if a PR doesn't merge."
- https://newsletter.posthog.com/p/what-we-learned-about-hiring-from (Apr 4, 2024): hire sourced from a GitHub stargazer (James Greenhill, ex-Uber).
- Ashby: 8 roles, all FullTime, none contractor/community.

## GitLab
- S-1 (sec.gov, filed Sept 17, 2021): "Kamil Trzcinski, a member of the wider community, contributed a better version of the GitLab CI application... Kamil ended up joining the company."
- https://about.gitlab.com/blog/2016/11/23/how-to-build-a-strong-dev-community/ (Nov 23, 2016), Amanda Folson on the monthly MVP list: "We actually tend to hire a lot of people who end up on that list, I think all of the top 20 now work at GitLab."
- https://about.gitlab.com/blog/contribute-to-open-source-land-jobs/ (2018-04-06): "almost a third of the first 40 engineers that GitLab hired were contributors to its codebase first".
- Secondary (community.inc, quoting Sid Sijbrandij SaaStr talk): "It might be tempting to hire from the wider community over time. You should focus your active recruiting efforts elsewhere to avoid depleting your wider community"; "90% of the hires we make are inbound. We get 3,000 applications every week."
- Contributors Program page: "This program is currently on hold." Greenhouse: 206 jobs; Senior Developer Advocate (8838202002); 3 contract roles are recruiting/sourcing, not engineering.

## Supabase
- https://supabase.com/contribute/about: "Paid contributions Top contributors get paid for their efforts. We pay a stipend that recognizes your time and expertise." and "Growth opportunities Room to grow from volunteer to paid contributor to paid employee."
- https://supabase.com/open-source/contributing/supasquad: same pathway language.
- postgresql.org message (Gurjeet Singh, 2021-08-31): "I'm very happy to announce that I now work for Supabase... They have hired me so that I can participate in, and contribute to the Postgres community."
- Ashby: 55 roles, all FullTime; 3 Developer Relations Engineer roles (published 2026-08-21) and an Event Programs Manager whose duties include "Support the SupaSquad advocate network".

## Cal.com
- No hiring-from-contributors statement. cal.com/jobs: Senior Product Design Engineer and IC4 Senior Full-Stack Engineer (dated Sep 24, 2026); design role bonus: "Open-source contributions and publicly available side projects we can view".
- https://algora.io/cal/bounties?status=open: "Completed bounties: 185"; "Open bounties: 2".
- https://cal.com/blog/seed: "8 core team members and another dozen part-time freelancers, and over 76 contributors from the community".
