Become a Verified Node Partner

Create a custom node that feels native on the n8n canvas. Tap into our automation community and enable easy connection to other n8n apps and data sources. Use our CLI and examples to build, then submit for verification to have your node appear in the UI and become part of the n8n ecosystem.

Why build a Verified node?

Turn your integration into a growth channel. Your verified node appears where users actively search for integrations and build the workflows that drive deep product engagement and stickiness.

  • Easy to install: Verified nodes are available directly in the n8n Editor, both Cloud and self-hosted
  • Discoverable: Show up in in-app search and get a dedicated page in the n8n Integration library
  • Trusted: A manual review process and a verified shield signal reliability to users
  • Get featured: Verified nodes are always on our radar for campaigns and content
