Frequently asked questions

What is the Webflow Partner Program?

The Webflow Partner Program is designed to help freelancers and agencies who build for clients on Webflow grow their businesses, deepen their Webflow expertise, and unlock new revenue opportunities.

All Webflow Partners earn commission on Site plans and add-ons, get access to product and sales enablement resources, and are invited to join our private partner community.

Certified and Premium Partners unlock additional benefits, including higher commission rates, listing in our partner directory, client-matching services, early access to beta features, and invites to exclusive events.
Note: If you're a content creator or influencer who regularly creates content about Webflow or web development, you may be a better fit for our Webflow Affiliates program. Learn more here ->

What is Webflow Foundations?

Foundations is the entry-level tier of the Webflow Partner Program, designed for freelancers and agencies that are actively building with Webflow and want to start growing with us right away.

What are Certified Partners?

Certified Partners are experienced freelancers and agencies with a proven track record building high-quality client sites on Webflow. Certified Partners get expanded benefits beyond the Foundations tier, including directory visibility and client-matching services.

Applicants to the Certified tier must pass an initial assessment, complete an application, and submit examples of client work that meet our quality standards.

How do I apply to become a Certified Partner?

Foundations members can apply for the Certified tier from the Partner Hub in their Webflow dashboard. Before applying, you’ll need to:
  1. Have an active Freelancer or Agency Workspace plan
  2. Pass a pre-qualification assessment to demonstrate your Webflow expertise
  3. Build at least three new client sites in Webflow as a Foundations member
  4. Submit your portfolio for review. It must include three client sites you’ve built from scratch in Webflow that demonstrate a range of skills and meet our site grading rubric.

Why are some Certified Partner applications declined?

Most rejections come down to unmet qualifications (like an inactive Workspace plan) or client site examples that don't meet our quality standards. Review our site grading rubric before applying to give your application the best chance of success.
