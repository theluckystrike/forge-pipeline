Agency tiers and benefits

Last reviewed on July 21, 2026

In this Guide

Automattic for Agencies tiers reward and support agencies based on their engagement with Automattic products. As you use our products, you generate Influenced Automattic Revenue (IAR). As you generate more IAR, you progress through tiers and unlock additional benefits. Our tiering system is designed to be inclusive, recognizing contributions regardless of which Automattic products you specialize in. In this guide, you will learn about each tier, its requirements, and the benefits you unlock as you progress.

How tiers work

When you reach a new tier, you keep that tier through the end of the following calendar year. At the start of each year, your tier is re-evaluated based on the revenue you generated in the previous year.

For example, if you reach Pro Partner at any point in 2026, you remain at Pro Partner through December 31, 2027, even if you do not generate additional revenue during that time. At the end of 2027, your tier may adjust up or down based on your 2027 results.

To keep your tier, you must continue to meet all eligibility requirements. Any grace period related to these requirements is provided at Automattic’s sole discretion.

Understanding Influenced Automattic Revenue (IAR)

Influenced Automattic Revenue (IAR) is the total revenue generated through Automattic products over one year through your agency’s direct influence. This includes confirmed client purchases and bulk agency purchases made within the Automattic for Agencies portal or tracked via the Automattic for Agencies plugin.

IAR is calculated as follows:

  • Product purchases and referrals: Each dollar spent counts as one dollar of IAR.
  • WooPayments referrals: $1 of IAR is recognized for every $100 in Total Payments Volume (TPV).

Tiers

Account Activated

Eligibility: Free to join with no minimum revenue requirements.

Requirements: Refer or purchase at least one Automattic product or bulk package each year to remain active.

Benefits:

  • A4A portal for streamlined client billing, program support, and product management
  • Reseller, revenue share, and referral earning opportunities
  • Access to Automattic sales teams for co-selling support
  • Program support and centralized product support
  • Access to Automattic community platforms
  • Product training and marketing materials

Agency Partner

Eligibility: Generate at least $1,200 in annual IAR across Automattic products, including WooPayments, WooCommerce, WordPress VIP hosting, WordPress.com, and Pressable.

Requirements:

  • Maintain outstanding client feedback.

Benefits:

Everything in Account Activated, plus:

  • Inclusion in agency directories (upon successful application – see our guide to applying for agency directories for more information)
  • Access to partner badges
  • Early access to new Automattic products and features

Pro Partner

Eligibility: Generate at least $5,000 in annual IAR.

Requirements:

  • Maintain outstanding client feedback.

Benefits:

Everything in Agency Partner, plus:

  • Development leads distributed by Partner Managers
  • Priority support
  • Assigned Partner Manager
  • Access to program advisory board for strategic insights
  • Co-marketing opportunities

VIP Pro Partner

Eligibility: Generate at least $100,000 in annual IAR. Entry is by invitation only.

Requirements:

  • Earn a listing in at least one Automattic agency directory (Pressable, WooCommerce, Jetpack, WordPress.com, or WordPress VIP).
  • Maintain outstanding client feedback.

Benefits:

Everything in Pro Partner, plus:

  • Annual credit for WooCommerce and Jetpack extensions
  • Higher earnings on VIP referrals

Premier Partner

Eligibility: Generate at least $250,000 in annual IAR. Entry is by invitation only.

Requirements:

  • Earn a listing in at least one Automattic agency directory (Pressable, WooCommerce, Jetpack, WordPress.com, or WordPress VIP).
  • Maintain outstanding client feedback.
  • Maintain three or more live enterprise-scale projects in the WordPress ecosystem, or three shared anchor customers.
  • Certify at least 30% of developers across Automattic certifications.

Benefits:

Everything in VIP Pro Partner, plus:

  • Free three-month Parse.ly trial
  • Marketing Development Funds (upon successful application)
