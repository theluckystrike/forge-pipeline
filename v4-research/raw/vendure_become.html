Earn your way up

It is earned, not bought. Your team gathers Business VXP (Vendure Experience Points) and climbs from Friend of Vendure to Vendure Partner.

Who we're looking for

Agencies and system integrators serious about building a Vendure practice. We weigh three things:

  • Experience delivering Vendure, or a strong track record in headless and composable commerce.
  • Commitment to building long-term commerce expertise, not one-off projects.
  • Collaboration, sharing learnings and growing alongside the Vendure ecosystem.

Fair opportunity by design: your commitment and quality of work matter more than your team size. Smaller studios compete on equal footing with larger agencies.

The process

How to join

Joining is light and fast, with a real person on the other side. Here is the path from downloading the guide to earning your first VXP in the partner portal.

1. Get the guide

Tell us a bit about your team and we'll email you the Vendure Partner Guide, with everything you need to size up the partnership. It takes a few minutes.

2. Check your inbox

The guide lands in your inbox right away, along with a link to book an intro call with our partner manager whenever you're ready.

3. Intro call

A short, no-pressure call with our partner manager to get to know each other, understand your goals, and make sure the partnership works both ways.

4. Onboard

We set you up in the partner portal and connect your GitHub, and you start earning VXP toward Friend of Vendure from day one.
