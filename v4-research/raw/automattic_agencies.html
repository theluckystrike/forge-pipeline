Everything you need to know

What is Automattic?

Automattic is the company behind WordPress.com, WooCommerce, WordPress VIP, Pressable, Jetpack, Tumblr, and more. It is known for its contribution to the open-source WordPress project and offers a range of products and services related to publishing, e-commerce, and security.

What is Automattic for Agencies?

Automattic for Agencies is the ultimate partnership to access agency pricing, referral earnings, premier support, and cutting-edge tooling for agencies using WordPress.com, Pressable, WooCommerce, Jetpack, and more.

How can our agency benefit from partnering with Automattic?

Agencies can get specialized support, economic incentives on Automattic products, training, and resources to improve product deployment and operational efficiencies.

How do I join?

Sign up for the program today to gain immediate access to new tools and resources crafted to enhance your work with clients.

Are there any costs associated with joining the program?

Joining the program has no associated costs. Progression is based on revenue generated within Automattic’s ecosystem.

Do I need to host my sites with Automattic to join?

No. You’re free to participate in the Automattic for Agencies program with whichever host you prefer.

What’s the difference between the Automattic Affiliate Program and Automattic for Agencies?

The Affiliate Program is for content creators earning commissions, while the Agencies Program is for agencies who sell custom, 1:1 solutions based on a client’s unique needs.
