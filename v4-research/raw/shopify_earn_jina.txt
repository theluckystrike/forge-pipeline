Title: How Partners earn on Shopify

URL Source: https://help.shopify.com/en/partners/getting-started/how-to-earn

Markdown Content:
As a Shopify Partner, you earn by referring merchants, building and transferring/launching stores, or developing apps and themes.

For answers to common questions about payout timing, revenue share calculations, lead statuses, and attribution rules, refer to the [_Shopify Partner earning model FAQ_](https://help.shopify.com/partners/help-support/faq/earnings).

Overview of the ways a Shopify Partner can earn, the amount earned for each activity, and how long each type of earning lasts
| Activity | Earnings | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Build and transfer/launch a merchant](https://help.shopify.com/en/partners/partner-program/how-to-earn#building-stores) | 20% of monthly base platform fee or subscription fee 1 + 0.1% of monthly eligible Gross Merchandise Volume 2 (GMV) | 4 years |
| [Build and transfer/launch a POS merchant](https://help.shopify.com/en/partners/partner-program/how-to-earn#shopify-pos) | 20% of monthly POS Payments Profit-Share | 4 years |
| [Build and transfer/launch a B2B merchant](https://help.shopify.com/en/partners/partner-program/how-to-earn#b2b) | 20% of monthly B2B Payments Profit-Share | 4 years |
| [Build and transfer/launch Shop Pay off Shopify](https://help.shopify.com/en/partners/partner-program/how-to-earn#shop-pay-off-shopify) | 0.1% of Gross Payment Volume (GPV) through Shop Pay off Shopify | 1 year |
| [Refer a new merchant](https://help.shopify.com/en/partners/partner-program/how-to-earn#referrals-and-upgrades) | Plus, Enterprise: $2,500 USD POS Pro: $500 USD | One-time |
| [Refer an existing merchant](https://help.shopify.com/en/partners/partner-program/how-to-earn#referrals-and-upgrades) | Upgrades to Plus: $2,500 USD | One-time |
| [Sell an app](https://help.shopify.com/en/partners/partner-program/how-to-earn#apps) | 100% of revenue (up to $1M USD revenue) 85% of revenue (over $1M USD revenue) | Per sale |
| [Sell a theme](https://help.shopify.com/en/partners/partner-program/how-to-earn#themes) | 85% of revenue | Per sale |

You can earn in two ways when helping a merchant get started on Shopify: by building and transferring/launching their store, or by referring them to Shopify.

What you earn, how to qualify, and when you get paid for building and transferring or launching a merchant store
|  | Build & Transfer/Launch |
| --- | --- |
| **What you earn** | **20%** of monthly base platform fee or subscription fee 1 + **0.1%** of monthly eligible GMV 2 |
| **How to qualify** | **Client transfer stores:** Create a client transfer store in the Partner Dashboard and transfer it to the merchant. The merchant must activate their plan through your transfer. **Plus deals sold alongside Shopify sales team:** Share an executed Statement of Work for a net new merchant. |
| **When you get paid** | Payouts are processed on a monthly basis. The two incentives are tracked and paid independently based on their specific triggers: * **20% of monthly subscription fee**: Begins the month of the first subscription payment (net of any account credits). * **0.1% of monthly eligible GMV share**: Begins as soon as the first dollar of eligible online GMV is processed. Please note that the 4-year earning term for each component begins independently once the first payment for that specific incentive is made to the partner. Additionally, once all eligibility terms are met, a standard 30-day hold is applied to the first payout. The funds are released shortly after this 30-day period concludes. |

Earn a one-time payout and profit share for every Shopify POS referral.

What you earn, how to qualify, and when you get paid for building and transferring or launching Shopify POS for a merchant
|  | POS Payments Profit-Share |
| --- | --- |
| **What you earn** | **20%** of Shopify POS Payments profit |
| **How to qualify** | Partner must: * Submit a Shopify POS [lead form](https://partners.shopify.com/current/partner_leads) from your Partner Dashboard Merchant must: * Be new to Shopify Payments on POS * Process more than $1,000 USD Gross Payments Volume through Shopify Payments on POS |
| **When you get paid** | Paid monthly, starting on the month of the first subscription payment. 4 year term begins once the first payment is made to a partner. _First payment is within **30 days** of all terms being met_ |

Earn a profit share for building and transferring/launching B2B for a merchant.

What you earn, how to qualify, and when you get paid for building and transferring or launching Shopify B2B for a merchant
|  | Build & Transfer/Launch |
| --- | --- |
| **What you earn** | **20%** of monthly B2B Payments profit |
| **How to qualify** | Partner must: * Submit a Shopify B2B [lead form](https://partners.shopify.com/current/partner_leads) from your Partner Dashboard Merchant must: * Be new to B2B |
| **When you get paid** | Paid monthly, starting on the month of the first B2B transaction through Shopify Payments. 4 year term begins once the first payment is made to a partner. _First payment is within **30 days** of all terms being met_ |

Earn a percentage of Gross Payments Volume (GPV) for building and transferring/launching Shop Pay off Shopify for a merchant.

What you earn, how to qualify, and when you get paid for building and transferring or launching Shop Pay off Shopify for a merchant
|  | Build & Transfer/Launch |
| --- | --- |
| **What you earn** | **0.1%** of Gross Payment Volume (GPV) through Shop Pay off Shopify |
| **How to qualify** | Merchant must: * Be new to Shop Pay off Shopify * Submit Statement of Work |
| **When you get paid** | Paid monthly, starting on the month of the first transaction through Shopify Payments. 1 year term begins once the first payment is made to a partner. _First payment is within **30 days** of all terms being met_ |

Earn a one-time payout for qualifying Plus referrals and upgrades.

What you earn, how to qualify, and when you get paid for a qualifying Shopify Plus or Enterprise referral or upgrade to Plus
|  | Referrals and Upgrades |
| --- | --- |
| **What you earn** | **$2,500 USD** one-time payout per qualifying Plus or Enterprise referral or upgrade to Plus |
| **How to qualify** | Partner must: * Submit a Shopify Plus [lead form](https://partners.shopify.com/current/partner_leads) in your Partner Dashboard Merchant must: * Not have contacted Shopify Sales before your submission. * Generate more than $5,000 USD Gross Merchandise Volume (GMV) on Shopify and have paid their first Plus subscription invoice * Referral must be qualified and result in a fully executed Plus or Enterprise agreement |
| **When you get paid** | _First payment is within **30 days** of all terms being met_ |

Earn a one-time payout for qualifying referrals to Shopify POS Pro.

What you earn, how to qualify, and when you get paid for a qualifying merchant referral to Shopify POS Pro
|  | Referrals and Upgrades |
| --- | --- |
| **What you earn** | **$500 USD** one-time payout |
| **How to qualify** | Partner must: * Submit a Shopify POS [lead form](https://partners.shopify.com/current/partner_leads) from your Partner Dashboard Merchant must (within 6 months of referral): * Be new to POS Pro and activate POS Pro * Transact 10+ days per month for 2 months * Generate more than $1,000 USD GMV per month |
| **When you get paid** | _First payment is within **30 days** of all terms being met_ |

### Apps

Your revenue share is based on your prior year's App Store earnings and company revenue.

Shopify App Store revenue share rates based on your prior year's App Store earnings and your total company revenue
| Your earnings profile | Revenue share |
| --- | --- |
| Earned **less than $20M USD** in the App Store AND company revenue **under $100M USD** | 100% on your first $1M USD earned 85% above $1M USD |
| Earned **$20M USD or more** in the App Store OR company revenue **$100M USD or more** | 85% on all revenue |

### Themes

Payout terms are subject to change. The final decision to grant revenue share, profit share, or one-time payouts is at Shopify's sole discretion.
