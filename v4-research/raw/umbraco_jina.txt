## Frequently Asked Questions

## Partnership structure & requirements

### What are the different partnership tiers (Silver, Gold, Platinum) and their specific requirements?

 

Umbraco offers three partnership tiers - Silver, Gold, and Platinum - each designed to match your agency’s level of experience and engagement with Umbraco.

Silver: Ideal for agencies getting started with Umbraco. Includes access to training, credits, and core marketing benefits.

Gold: For agencies delivering more advanced Umbraco projects, with higher training requirements and added sales and marketing perks.

Platinum: Reserved for highly specialized Umbraco agencies focused on enterprise projects, cloud implementations, and strategic collaboration with Umbraco HQ.

Each tier includes annual training seat requirements and provides credits, discounts, and unique co-marketing opportunities.

### Can I upgrade or downgrade my partnership tier during the contract period?

 

Yes, you can upgrade your partnership at any time during your subscription year to access more benefits and higher credit discounts. Downgrades typically take effect at the time of annual renewal and are handled in close collaboration with your Partner Manager.

### Is there a minimum commitment period for partnership?

 

Yes, all partnerships run on a yearly auto-renewed subscription. This gives you time to ramp up your projects, leverage credits, complete training, and make the most of your partner benefits.

### Can I pause or cancel my partnership if needed?

 

If your business circumstances change, you can cancel your renewal at the end of the subscription period. Pausing a partnership mid-term isn't currently supported, but we recommend contacting your Partner Manager to explore flexible options based on your situation.

## Training & certification

### How many training certifications are required for each partnership tier?

 

Each partnership tier has specific active certification requirements to maintain status:

*   **Silver:** 1 active Certified Professional (attained by passing the Fundamentals exam).

*   **Gold:** 1 active Certified Expert.

*   **Platinum:** 1 active Certified Master **OR** 2 active Certified Experts.

Certifications are valid for **two years**, ensuring your team stays up-to-date with the latest Umbraco versions and best practices. Developers can progress through the certification paths at their own pace, moving from Professional up to Master status by completing the required training courses and passing the exams.

### Do I need to complete training before becoming a partner, or can it be done after?

 

You can join the program and complete training during your partnership year. Umbraco provides onboarding guidance and helps you plan training so you meet certification requirements within the partnership term.

### Is training available online or does it require in-person attendance?

 

Training is available in flexible formats, including:

- On-demand online courses for self-paced learning

- Live online training sessions

- On-site custom training (optional, paid via credits)

This ensures you can train your team regardless of location or time zone.

## Credits & ROI

### How does the credit system work and what can credits be used for?

 

Each partner tier includes a bundle of credits that can be used for:

- Commercial products (e.g., Umbraco Cloud, Forms, Commerce, Workflow)

- Training courses

- Support services like architectural advising, code review, or hypercare

- Custom onboarding or enterprise support for your clients

Credits give you flexibility to scale your Umbraco capabilities on your terms.

### What happens if I don't use all my credits before renewal?

 

Unused credits are automatically rolled over into your next partnership period, so you won’t lose them when you renew. However, please note that credits expire two years from their original purchase date. This ensures flexibility across renewal cycles while keeping credit usage up to date.

### Can I use credits to pay for partnership renewal fees?

 

Credits cannot be used to pay for your annual partnership subscription. However, they can be used for almost everything else, including additional training, support, or commercial product usage.

### How quickly can I recoup my partnership investment?

 

Many partners see a return on investment within just a few projects. With no license fees for the CMS, discounted access to add-ons, and sales support from Umbraco HQ, you can recoup your investment quickly, often within the first 1–2 projects depending on scope.

### Is partnership financially viable if I don't have many Umbraco projects yet?

 

Yes. The Silver tier is designed for agencies just starting with Umbraco. The included training, credits, and marketing support help you quickly build your capability and visibility to attract more clients. The ROI grows with your commitment to the platform.

## Support

### How does lead generation and distribution work for partners?

 

Umbraco actively promotes certified partners on [umbraco.com](http://umbraco.com/)and through strategic campaigns. Leads are:

- Referred through the Partner Finder

- Shared based on project relevance, regional fit, and partner tier

- Supported by co-selling efforts with Umbraco HQ for commercial products

Higher tiers receive greater exposure and lead potential.

### What marketing materials and co-marketing opportunities are available?

 

All partners receive access to:

- Official Umbraco Partner badge

Yes. Umbraco supports a global partner network with access to:

- Regional Partner Managers

- A growing network of local language resources

We aim to make sure you're supported, wherever you're located.
