Become a Sponsor

Platinum Sponsors

Become a platinum sponsor with a monthly donation of $2,000 USD and get your Open Collective or GitHub avatar image on our READMEs on GitHub and the home page of eslint.org. We will also tweet a thank you from our Twitter account (over 38,000 followers).

Gold Sponsors

Become a gold sponsor with a monthly donation of $1,000 USD and get your Open Collective or GitHub avatar image on our README on GitHub and the front page of eslint.org. We will also tweet a thank you from our Twitter account (over 38,000 followers).

Silver Sponsors

Become a silver sponsor with a monthly donation of $500 USD and get your Open Collective or GitHub avatar image on our README on GitHub and the front page of eslint.org.

Bronze Sponsors

Become a bronze sponsor with a monthly donation of $200 USD and get your Open Collective or GitHub avatar image on our README on GitHub and the front page of eslint.org.

Technology Sponsors

Technology sponsors allow us to use their products and services for free as part of a contribution to the open source ecosystem and our work.
