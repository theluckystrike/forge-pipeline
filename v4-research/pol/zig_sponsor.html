Mission Statement

The mission of the Zig Software Foundation is to promote, protect, and advance the Zig programming language, to support and facilitate the growth of a diverse and international community of Zig programmers, and to provide education and guidance to students, teaching the next generation of programmers to be competent, ethical, and to hold each other to high standards.

ZSF is a 501(c)(3) non-profit corporation.

Sponsorship

By donating to ZSF, you are funding development of the Zig Programming Language and its ecosystem, which in turn benefits the greater open source community. Zig community members have landed bug fixes in LLVM, Wine, QEMU, musl libc, GDB and others.

ZSF makes efficient use of monetary resources. While we want to stay small, we also want to provide opportunity for unpaid volunteers to become contractors who can earn a living with their contributions.
