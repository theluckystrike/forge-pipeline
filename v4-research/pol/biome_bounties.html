Enterprise Support

Biome has an active community and a team of Maintainers, Core Contributors, and Leads, all ready to support our users.

Some of our contributors are freelancers, and may offer their (paid) services for organizations that are looking to adopt Biome. If you need commercial help with Biome, please reach out to us!

Contact us on Discord

Reach out to us through the Community > #funding channel for enquiries related to commercial support.

Create an issue on GitHub

Create an issue and choose Commercial Support Request and we'll get back to you.

Would you like to support Biome?

Biome is an open-source project and can also use your support. If you hire a contributor to work on Biome for 3 months or more, you automatically apply for sponsorship benefits.

More sponsorship options are available through OpenCollective and GitHub Sponsors.

Support requests are subject to contributor availability. For terms and conditions, please see our Governance document.
