Description

Who we are:

Immeo is a leading digital consultancy headquartered in Copenhagen, Denmark, helping organizations navigate complexity and build the resilience needed to thrive in a world of constant change. With more than 160 specialists across strategy, architecture, design, software engineering, data, cloud, and business transformation, we partner with both private and public sector organizations to create lasting business value.

We combine deep business understanding with technical expertise to design, build, and operate digital solutions that strengthen competitiveness, improve customer experiences, and enable sustainable growth. Our expertise spans digital commerce, customer portals, product information management (PIM), business applications, data and AI, cloud platforms, enterprise integration, and custom software development.

At Immeo, we believe that successful digital transformation is about more than technology. We take a holistic approach that connects people, strategy, processes, and systems to create solutions that deliver value today while preparing organizations for tomorrow's challenges and opportunities. We call this digital resilience - the ability to adapt, innovate, and grow in an ever-changing business environment.

By combining strategic insight with modern architectures, cloud-native platforms, API-first integration, and high-quality software engineering, we help organizations establish flexible and future-ready digital foundations. Our collaborative approach ensures that every solution is aligned with business objectives and designed to deliver measurable outcomes, long-term scalability, and continuous innovation.
