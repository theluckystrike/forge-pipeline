Medusa Cases

DBot Software uses Medusa's open, modular framework to develop reliable eCommerce systems for B2C, B2B, and marketplace environments. We build solutions that connect seamlessly with existing business systems while maintaining flexibility and long-term maintainability.

DD Bricks Marketplace (Ongoing): Developing a custom Medusa-based platform for DD Bricks, a major global seller of used LEGO® parts. The solution integrates MedusaJS, Next.js, and warehouse management systems to manage thousands of SKUs with flexible pricing and automated multi-warehouse fulfillment.

Automated Multi-Storefront System for Funeral Flower Ordering: Contributed to a Medusa-based multi-shop setup for a provider of digital funeral services, giving families a more personal way to order flowers for individual ceremonies. The system automatically generates a dedicated storefront for each funeral event, complete with curated arrangements, a respectful and streamlined checkout, and a real-time connection to the provider's industry-specific ERP. DBot supported ongoing development, stability improvements, and refinements to the Medusa–ERP integration as the system moved toward a reliable, scalable release.

Dual-Account Digital Wallet for an Automotive Marketplace: Built a custom dual-account wallet on top of Medusa that integrates directly into a multi-vendor marketplace checkout. Customers pay seamlessly from their wallet, with promotional credits automatically applied before their own funds, while vendors still receive correct payouts and the platform keeps customer funds, incentives, and settlements cleanly separated behind the scenes. The build included a full transaction ledger, OTP-based wallet activation, configurable referral and top-up campaigns, wallet refunds, and an admin interface for managing balances and promotions, creating a scalable foundation for future growth without adding complexity for customers or vendors.
