About Rigby

Rigby is an eCommerce development agency building tailored commerce solutions for modern, complex business models. We specialize in creating B2B, B2C, and multi-vendor marketplaces that reflect how our clients actually sell.

Our team of 45+ developers works with clients across North America, Europe, Asia and the Middle East to deliver platforms that scale, integrate deeply, and unlock new business models.

We are problem solvers, innovators, and eCommerce specialists with a passion for tackling complex challenges. Our expertise lies in backend-heavy architectures, custom logic, and building solutions that other teams find too difficult. We’ve been recognized as top-rated Medusa.js developers on Clutch in both 2023 and 2024.

We’re not just implementers — we actively shape the Medusa ecosystem by creating starters, plugins, and continuously contributing to the platform.

We’ve also been featured by Medusa for scaling Patyna’s vintage marketplace, building the Solace storefront starter, and migrating legacy stores from Magento to Medusa.

We’re also the creators of MercurJS — an open-source, modular platform for launching custom B2B and B2C marketplaces on Medusa. Designed to remove technical overhead and accelerate time-to-market, Mercur comes with pre-built vendor tools, multi-vendor cart and checkout, and a flexible architecture ready for custom flows.

If your business doesn’t fit into a template — we’ll build one that does.

Medusa Cases

Rigby has worked extensively with Medusa, leveraging its open-source, modular architecture to build multi-vendor marketplaces, B2B procurement platforms, and subscription-first digital products. Our solutions prioritize performance, scalability, and seamless integration with third-party tools like ERP systems, PIMs, or headless CMS platforms.

Highlighted Case: Launching a Custom B2B Marketplace

A leading supplier platform in the Middle East partnered with us to launch a custom-built B2B Medusa marketplace designed to support thousands of restaurants, hotels, and caterers. The project included a full commerce backend, vendor portal, and native mobile app to streamline bulk ordering and real-time availability across 40,000+ SKUs.

We implemented custom pricing logic, seller-specific catalogs, multi-vendor carts, and mobile-first workflows tailored to the food & beverage industry.

The result was a unified marketplace that digitized traditional procurement flows and provided a scalable foundation for expansion.

Other Medusa Cases

  • Subscription-Based SaaS Platform: Built a subscription-first SaaS marketplace with license automation, flexible billing, and vendor onboarding.
  • Grocery B2C Marketplace: Developed an AI-enhanced grocery platform with ERP sync, catalog automation, and cross-chain vendor logic.
  • DTC Optics Brand: Delivered a fast Medusa-powered storefront with a modern UI and clean purchasing flows.
  • Digital Rental Platform: Created a localized service marketplace with upsells and host dashboards.
  • Wholesale B2B Store: Built a tailored procurement system with RFQs, role-based access, and regional pricing.
  • POS for Secondhand Retail: Designed a point-of-sale system with live inventory, payout flows, and store reporting.
  • Multi-Tenant B2B2C Marketplace: Built a logistics-heavy marketplace with storefront management, service integration, and AI-based ordering.

All cases were built with modularity and business alignment in mind — reflecting real-world workflows, not templates.
