We drive success for our clients through four core tenets:

  1. Experts of the Web: Our seasoned professionals leverage cutting-edge frameworks and proven best practices to accelerate your time-to-market. This means you can quickly capitalize on opportunities and stay ahead of competitors.
  2. Quick Iterations: Rapid prototyping and iterative testing allow us to validate your project’s viability swiftly. You gain actionable insights early, reducing risk and helping you confidently seize new business opportunities.
  3. Exceptional Quality: We deliver meticulously crafted solutions, minimizing bugs and rework. Our commitment to quality enhances your customers’ trust, strengthens your brand’s reputation, and ultimately boosts your bottom line.

Medusa Cases

Lambda Curry excels at extending Medusa’s capabilities to create highly customized, impactful e-commerce solutions. Below are some of our signature Medusa-powered projects:

Highlighted Case: Ashley x Descend (SaySo)

Lambda Curry and SaySo partnered to build Ashley x Descend, an innovative descending-auction platform powered by Medusa. The platform reimagines clearance sales through real-time price drops, instant “Buy Now” purchases, and AI-driven product categorization, creating a gamified, engaging shopping experience that significantly boosts customer interaction and average order values. By moving inventory directly from regional warehouses, Ashley opened new revenue streams, slashed logistics costs, and reduced environmental impact.

Lambda Curry’s expertise enabled us to meet our ambitious deadlines and goals. By implementing best practices and upskilling our team on the Medusa platform, we managed to launch our e-commerce MVP within three months.

Plugins & Starters

Medusa 2.0 Starter Kit

Our Lambda Curry Medusa 2.0 Starter empowers developers to rapidly build scalable e-commerce experiences. Built as a Turborepo monorepo, it integrates a flexible Medusa 2 backend with a performant Remix frontend.

Key Features:

  • Dynamic Category Pages: Fully customizable, interactive storefront experiences.
  • Extensive Product Management: Effortlessly handle unlimited products, categories, and variations.
  • Coffee Roast-Themed Storefront: Engaging and easily adaptable to any niche.

This starter is designed not only for a quick launch but also to grow alongside your business, providing a reliable foundation for evolving e-commerce needs.
