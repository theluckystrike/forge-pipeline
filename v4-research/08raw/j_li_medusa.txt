We launched Medusa Search this week, and let me tell you why this product is more than just fast and cheap search for your shoppers. The part I'm really excited about is what we can use it for in domains outside regular search, especially for powering AI agents. Giving an agent access to your APIs or database is not enough. It needs a fast way to find the information relevant to the task at hand. And it needs to do it efficiently to not blow up the context (your bill). Medusa Search gives agents both keyword and semantic search, and lets you make any data in your stack searchable, even data outside your commerce platform. For example, we've built customer service agents for several customers that use Medusa Search to store historical Zendesk tickets alongside commerce data like products and orders. When a new ticket comes in, the agent can search commerce data to answer questions about what is happening in Medusa. But it can also look up previous tickets to see if the question has already been answered. This makes resolution faster, cheaper, and more consistent. Zendesk tickets is just one example. The same pattern applies to any data an agent needs to do its job. Search is an important primitive for agentic commerce, and this is why we built it directly into Medusa Cloud.
