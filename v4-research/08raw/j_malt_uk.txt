## About us

Founded in 2013 by Vincent Huguet (CEO), Malt is the leading freelance marketplace in Europe. Over 100,000 companies of all sizes find the external talent they need in Malt’s community of more than one million freelancers.   
  
With the 2022 acquisition of Comatch, a marketplace specializing in independent consultants, Malt is now present in 9 countries and regions (Belgium, France, Germany, the Netherlands, the Nordics, Spain, Switzerland, the UAE, and the UK). Investments from Eurazeo, Goldman Sachs Asset Management, Serena, Isai and BPI Large Ventures highlight the support for and confidence in Malt’s vision.
