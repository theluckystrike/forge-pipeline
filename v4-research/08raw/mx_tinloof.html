Description


Who we are

Tinloof is a studio that designs and develops marketing sites, online stores, and web apps.

Medusa Cases

Tinloof builds B2C and B2B commerce on Medusa, from bespoke made-to-order catalogs to merchandise and ticketing. We also created Munchies, a feature-rich B2C starter showcasing Medusa's flexibility.

Highlighted case: More than a template

We developed a full-fledged cookies brand, Munchies, elevating the starter from generic to genuinely engaging.

On top of that, we:

  • Built a robust design system including code snippets, facilitating easy customization and consistent styling across the platform.
  • Expanded PDPs with rich content, including testimonials, and product recommendations for a comprehensive shopping experience.
  • Made the store a reusable starter that leverages Figma to enable rapid theme changes, like going from a cookies brand to a furniture one.
  • Made the starter raises the bar in e-commerce, offering unique experiences for each customer with instant page loads and interactions.

Other Medusa Cases

  • Brand Your: a self-serve storefront for a UK B2B brand selling custom-branded, made-to-order food packaging, with hundreds of configurable product options across the UK and US.
  • FC Basel: a DTC platform combining football merchandise and ticketing for the club.

Medusa Specializations

At Tinloof we believe that having a holistic standpoint enables us to offer harmonious experiences.

Rather than passing disjointed pieces that are hard to glue from one agency to the other, we curated our offering to focus on:

  1. Branding: from strategy to visual identity, our goal is to create memorable and distinct identities.
  2. Web design: we retain a focus on usability, responsiveness, accessibility, and pleasant aesthetics.
  3. Development: we build performant interfaces backed by services that empower brands to operate at scale.
