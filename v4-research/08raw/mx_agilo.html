Medusa Cases

Agilo crafts scalable platforms with Medusa’s modular tools. From seamless integrations to custom solutions, we help businesses thrive with flexibility and speed.

Highlighted Case: Scaling Tekla’s Multi-Channel Success

Tekla partnered with Agilo to transform its ecommerce and retail operations.

We revamped their Medusa-powered webshop with a sleek checkout experience, seamless ERP integration, and a migration from Stripe to Adyen for global payments. Additionally, we migrated their frontend architecture from Gatsby to Next.js, ensuring higher performance, scalability, and enhanced developer experience.

For their first Copenhagen store, we built a custom React Native POS system, unifying online and offline channels with real-time inventory management.

The result? A seamless, scalable ecosystem that powers Tekla’s growth while staying true to its commitment to quality and design.

Not only have we seen great results from our collaboration, but we also feel that Agilo is an extended arm of our organization in Split. Our partnership now extends beyond a professional relationship, reinforcing the trust, support and availability that a great partnership needs.

Kristoffer Windall Juhl

·

CEO & Founder at TEKLA
