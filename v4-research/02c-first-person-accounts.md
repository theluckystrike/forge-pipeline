Adjacent: daenney (PuppetDB work led to Spotify offer, 2014); scarface_74 (HN 2025-03-19, "third highest contributor on the project" gave "insta offers" from consulting firms, https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=43414821); x64k (Lobsters 2024: trivial kernel driver "cinched me two contracts").

Company-side confirmations: Appwrite (https://appwrite.io/blog/post/the-evolution-of-team-appwrite, 2024-01-23: Christy Jacob "one of the very first active contributors... joined the team as a Founding Engineer"); Home Assistant (2025-09-17: new foundation hires "were active community members"); Novu (2022-05-11: "Novu recruited many of our contributors to work full-time on Novu"); GitLab (2018: "almost a third of the first 40 engineers"); Directus (https://directus.com/about: "Many of our hires started as contributors."); Supabase ("Room to grow from volunteer to paid contributor to paid employee."); Origin Protocol (HN 2019: "hired several engineers that started as open-source contributors").

Null results: bitbasher (HN 2025: "I have never received a job offer because of a specific open source contribution"); rleigh (HN 2019: none of 3 jobs used OSS work directly); pabs3; Khalid Warsame (2023-03-12, Riverpod contributor #83: "Most employers care about what you can do for them, not what you have done for an online community, unless it's their own community or target market."); Kiran Krishnan (3 Supabase integrations merged, no job mentioned); Armand Philippot (Astro core, no pay); Prithvi S (OpenSearch maintainer, no hire); Blind 2018 thread ("vast majority of companies and hiring managers in SV won't care").

Pattern: time from first contribution to offer ranged from under a week (Backpack) to 3 months (Zed x2) to 10-13 months (Zed, GitLab, OpenNMS, Camunda). All fast hires were at small VC-backed companies whose founders personally watch the repo. Sustained volume in ONE repo (20 to 40+ PRs) is the common feature of the 3-month cases.
